We are incredibly proud to announce that our very own competitive programming cohort, **Team Null Pointers**, has secured the Grand Prize at the 2026 National Code and Hardware Hackathon held in Metro Manila this past weekend.

Competing against 45 other university teams, our representatives demonstrated exceptional problem-solving skills, grace under pressure, and mastery of both software and hardware integration.

## The Winning Project

The prompt for this year's hackathon was to create an accessible smart-city solution focusing on disaster risk reduction. 

Team Null Pointers developed **FloodSense**, an automated, low-cost early warning system. The architecture utilized:
1.  ESP32 Microcontrollers linked to ultrasonic water-level sensors.
2.  A custom LoRaWAN mesh network to ensure data transmission even when cellular towers fail.
3.  A responsive React dashboard that visualizes the water level data in real-time.

### Judges' Feedback

The judging panel, which included senior engineers from AWS and local telecommunications firms, praised the project for its practical applicability in the Philippines. 

> "While many teams built impressive software dashboards, the Mapúa team went the extra mile by engineering a custom hardware mesh network. That understanding of physical infrastructure is what set them apart." — Lead Judge

Congratulations to the team members: Mark Rivera, Sarah Lin, and David Chen. Your hard work and late nights in the lab have truly paid off, and you've brought great pride to the Computer Engineering department!
