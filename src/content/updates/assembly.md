*   **Workshop Schedule:** Announcing the dates for our highly anticipated Embedded Systems and ReactJS bootcamps.
*   **Merch Reveal:** First look at the new departmental lanyards and polo shirts.
*   **Open Floor:** A Q&A session with the executive officers and faculty advisers.
